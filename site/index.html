01 · Select. Click. Listen.

A speaker appears next to anything you select.

Click it and the text is read to you, sentence by sentence, with the spoken sentence highlighted on the page. Read whole pages, open long articles in a calm Smart Reader, queue them up like an audiobook.

  • 28 English voices, speed 0.5× to 3× with natural pitch
  • Keeps playing when you switch tabs
  • Export any text as a WAV file

02 · नेपाली पनि

Reads Nepali news, too.

Nepali text is detected sentence by sentence and read by a Nepali voice, so a page that mixes Nepali and English just works. Try it on onlinekhabar, ekantipur, setopati or BBC Nepali.

03 · Private by design

Your text never leaves your device.

Speech is generated locally by open models. No accounts, no analytics, no tracking. The voice downloads once, is checked against a pinned checksum, and then works offline.

04 · Runs on your machine

Fast, on any computer.

Kokoro-82M runs in multithreaded WebAssembly everywhere, and on the GPU through WebGPU where it is available, about four times faster than real time. Nepali voices run faster than real time on any CPU.
